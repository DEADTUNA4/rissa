Why not synthetic counters?

Synthetic bytes([i%256]) is not sensor data. Real NOAA binary is <I fff> structs (ts + temp/hum/pres) with jitter - needs DELTA_ZIGZAG + SHUFFLE. Use your 1MB+ raw dump in corpora/.
