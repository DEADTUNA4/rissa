Full pipeline I/O on HDD is ~0.6× CPU speed (HDD cannot feed 12 workers — use 2-3, imap reuse pool). Synthetic Counter 5KB: zlib 324 → DELTA+zstd 71 -78%. Full PHASE3_REPORT.md · v3 128K results: NOAA 500K 238K SHUFFLE_8, Yellow 2M tie, Silesia dickens 714K RAW.

Why not synthetic counters?

Synthetic bytes([i%256]) is not sensor data. Real NOAA binary is <I fff> structs with jitter — needs DELTA_ZIGZAG + SHUFFLE. Use your 1MB+ raw dump in corpora/.
